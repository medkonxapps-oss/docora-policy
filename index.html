Privacy Policy — Docora

Last updated: 12 October 2026  ·  Contact: medkonxapps@gmail.com

Docora is a document, image, video and audio viewer for Android.

Your files

Docora reads files on your device only to list and display them. Files, file names and file contents are never uploaded or shared by the app. Favourites, recent files and settings are stored locally on your device.

Storage permission

Docora asks for storage ("All files access" on Android 11+) so it can find documents and media in any folder. You can revoke it any time in system Settings; the app then cannot list your files.

Advertising

Docora shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID, IP address and device/usage data to serve and measure ads. In the EEA/UK you are asked for consent first (Google UMP). You can reset or opt out of the Advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data we collect

We do not run our own servers and do not collect personal data ourselves. Only Google AdMob (above) processes data for advertising.

Permissions

  • All files access / storage: to find and open documents, images, videos and audio stored on your device.
  • Notifications / foreground service (media playback): to keep audio playing in the background with notification controls.
  • Internet / network state: only to load ads.

Children

Docora is not directed to children under 13.
